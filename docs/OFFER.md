# The Grand Slam Offer (Hormozi framework)

> The cold-call offer rebuilt as a complete Alex Hormozi "$100M Offers" offer.
> This is the *thinking* behind what we say; the spoken words stay fixed in
> `apps/web/lib/script.ts` (v3). Amounts in brackets are the lead's own
> calculated band from `savings.ts` — 240.000–400.000 kr is only the fallback.

---

## 1. Avatar & dream outcome

**Avatar:** the owner of a small Danish local business (typisk 1–15 ansatte),
found in CVR, who does admin at night and weekends. They didn't start the
business to do paperwork, chase invoices, retype the same data, or answer the
same phone questions — but that's where their evenings go.

**Dream outcome (what we sell, in their words):**

> Få [240.000–400.000] kroner om året og timerne tilbage — uden at ansætte,
> uden at lære nyt software, og uden at betale én krone, før det virker.

The dream is not "automation". It's *run the business, not the paperwork* —
quantified in kroner from **their own filed accounts**, so the number is
theirs, not ours.

## 2. The value equation

Hormozi: **Value = (Dream outcome × Perceived likelihood) / (Time delay ×
Effort & sacrifice)**. Maximize the top, crush the bottom.

| Lever | How this offer moves it |
|---|---|
| **Dream outcome** | A concrete DKK band per year, forever — not "efficiency". The band comes from their bruttofortjeneste/resultat, so it scales with the business they actually run. |
| **Perceived likelihood** | (1) The number is derived from *their own* filed accounts. (2) We *watch first* for 30 days and show a written estimate before building anything. (3) They approve the estimate before any build. (4) They pay only on **documented** results. Every proof step happens *before* money moves. |
| **Time delay** | The show-up bonus pays out **5 minutes after the first meeting** (2 quick wins ready). The 30-day map arrives in 30 days. Savings start the day the build ships. Nothing in the offer asks them to wait for value until after payment — payment is the *last* event. |
| **Effort & sacrifice** | "Ikke fysisk — remote." No new software to learn (we fit systems to how they already work), no hiring, no up-front cash, no risk of paying for something that doesn't work. Their total effort: one 10-minute meeting + letting us watch. |

## 3. Problems → solutions → the stack

Hormozi's method: list every problem between the avatar and the dream outcome,
turn each into a solution, give each solution a named deliverable.

| Problem (what stops them) | Solution | Named deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| "Jeg ved ikke, hvor pengene og tiden render hen." | We follow the business remotely for 30 days and map every leak. | **30-Dages Pengelækage-Kortlægningen** (an ops audit consultancies charge 25–50.000 kr for — here: gratis) |
| "Jeg har ikke tid til at fikse det selv." | Done-for-you: we design *and build* the systems. | **Bygningen** — done-for-you, ikke gør-det-selv |
| "Virker det overhovedet for *min* slags forretning?" | The savings estimate is computed from their own filed accounts and reviewed together before any build. | **Besparelses-Estimatet** (skriftligt, gennemgås sammen, de godkender før vi bygger) |
| "Konsulenter tager penge up front og forsvinder." | Zero up front. Payment only after build, only on documented result. | **Ingenting-før-det-virker-modellen** |
| "Hvad med skjulte løbende gebyrer?" | One payment: 20 % of documented year-one savings. After that the saving is theirs forever. | **Én-gangs-afregningen** (20 %, én gang) |
| "Jeg gider ikke lære et nyt system." | Systems are fitted to their existing workflow; nothing new to learn. | **Passer-til-jer-princippet** |
| "Hvad hvis I ikke finder noget?" | Then nothing is built and nothing is owed — and they keep the map. | **Garantien** (se §5) |

## 4. Price & terms — anchored

- **0 kr** for the 30-day map. **0 kr** for the written estimate. **0 kr**
  until the build is live and the saving is documented.
- Then **20 % of documented year-one savings — once.** Year two onward: 100 %
  theirs, forever.
- **The anchor (say the ratio out loud):** with a [240.000–400.000] band the
  one-time fee is [48.000–80.000] kr — paid *from money we already got back
  for them* — to keep [240.000–400.000] kr **every year after**. That's 5:1 in
  year one and infinite after. They are never out of pocket: the fee is a
  slice of recovered money, not new spend.

This is Hormozi's *divergent pricing*: we don't price the work (hours), we
price the outcome — which makes the price a fraction of delivered value by
construction, at any deal size.

## 5. Guarantee — better than money-back

A money-back guarantee refunds risk. This offer never collects the money, so
there is nothing to refund:

> **"Sparer vi dig ingenting, betaler du ingenting."**

Spelled out as the full risk-reversal stack:
1. Free to look (30 days, remote, no disruption).
2. Written estimate before any build — they can walk away with the map.
3. Nothing billed until built **and** measured.
4. The fee is capped at 20 % of what was *actually* documented — if the saving
   comes in lower, the fee comes in lower.

Optional amplifier (use when a lead is warm but hesitant):
> "Finder vi ikke mindst 100.000 kr i årlig lækage på de 30 dage, siger jeg
> det ærligt, vi går igen — og du beholder hele kortlægningen."

## 6. Scarcity — real, not theater

**2 pladser.** True capacity: a 30-day remote follow is attention-intensive,
so we run at most two at a time. Hormozi's rule is that scarcity must be real
— ours is, so say *why*: "vi følger kun 2 virksomheder ad gangen, ellers kan
vi ikke gøre det ordentligt." When both slots fill, the next opening is a full
30-day cycle away.

## 7. Urgency — the leak is per month

The dream outcome has a burn rate. [240.000–400.000] kr/år is
**[20.000–33.000] kr every month** that keeps leaving while they think about
it. The cost of "jeg vender tilbage" is one month of the band — more than the
entire risk of saying yes (which is zero). Pair with the slot cycle: waiting
doesn't just delay the saving, it risks the slot.

## 8. Bonuses

1. **Show-up bonus (paid before they commit to anything):** "når du møder op,
   har jeg allerede 2 ting klar til dig, som sparer dig penge og tid 5
   minutter efter mødet." Value delivered *before* the sale — Hormozi's
   fastest trust-builder.
2. **The map is theirs:** even if they never proceed, the 30-day
   Pengelækage-Kortlægning stays with them. The free thing is independently
   valuable.
3. **The estimate meeting:** a walk-through of exactly what the fix is worth,
   in writing — useful even if they build it themselves.

## 9. Name (MAGIC formula)

**M**agnetic reason: 2 åbne pladser · **A**vatar: [branche] (spoken per lead:
"frisørsaloner", "tømrervirksomheder"…) · **G**oal: [240.000–400.000] kr
tilbage · **I**nterval: 30 dage · **C**ontainer: Kortlægning + Garanti.

> **"30-Dages Pengelækage-Kortlægningen for [branche] — find
> [240.000–400.000] kr, eller det koster ingenting."**

## 10. The whole offer in one spoken paragraph (Danish)

How the pieces compress back into 30 seconds on the phone:

> "Jeg har fundet dig i CVR-registret, og jeg tror, du vil kunne være et godt
> fit til 1 af de 2 pladser, vi har åbne lige nu. Det, vi gør, er helt
> simpelt: vi sparer [branche] en masse penge — realistisk set, for dig,
> mellem [240.000 og 400.000] kroner årligt. Det er tal fra jeres egne
> regnskaber, ikke noget jeg finder på. Vi følger jeres virksomhed i 30 dage
> — remote — og finder præcis, hvor pengene render hen. Det koster ingenting
> at kigge. Finder vi noget, viser vi dig sort på hvidt, hvad det er værd,
> *før* vi bygger noget. Og du betaler først, når det er bygget og virker: 20
> % af det, vi kan dokumentere, vi har sparet dig på et år — én gang. Resten
> er dit, hvert år, for altid. Sparer vi dig ingenting, betaler du
> ingenting."

## 11. Where the v3 script already delivers this — and the gaps

| Hormozi element | In script v3? | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Quantified dream outcome | ✅ savings band in the pitch | Lead's own band, fallback 240–400k |
| Scarcity (2 slots) | ✅ "1 af de 2 pladser" | Consider adding the *reason* ("vi følger kun 2 ad gangen") — real scarcity lands harder with the why |
| Time-collapse bonus | ✅ show-up PS ("2 ting klar … 5 minutter efter") | Correctly placed *after* booking as a show-up lock |
| Risk reversal / guarantee | ⚠️ only surfaces if they ask "hvad koster det?" | The strongest line we own ("sparer vi dig ingenting…") is hidden behind a question the lead has to ask. Candidate for the split-test bank as an 8th follow-up. |
| Fee-vs-return anchor | ✅ `feeLine` when savings exist | Only spoken with real accounts data — correct |
| Urgency (monthly burn) | ❌ not in script | "[20–33.000] kr hver måned, mens du tænker" — candidate split-test line |
| Proof ("jeres egne regnskaber") | ⚠️ implicit | When `basis === "accounts"`, saying "det er tal fra jeres eget regnskab" is the cheapest likelihood-boost available |

Script wording changes go through the user verbatim (v3 rule) — this table is
a menu, not an instruction.
